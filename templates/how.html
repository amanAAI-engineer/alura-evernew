How it works

Clean. Protect. Preserve.

Cleaning removes visible dirt. ALURA EverNew adds a layer of antimicrobial surface protection after cleaning, so the interior stays fresher and better protected between deep cleans.

Bacti Barrier™ Technology

A protective layer that keeps working

Once dry, the treatment forms a protective antimicrobial surface layer that keeps eliminating the microbes that cause deterioration and discoloration of surfaces.

Cleaned, but untreated

Invisible microbes stay on the surface, release biochemicals and slowly cause stains and discoloration.

Cleaned and treated with EverNew

The antimicrobial surface layer keeps eliminating microbes, protecting the surface from staining and discoloration.
